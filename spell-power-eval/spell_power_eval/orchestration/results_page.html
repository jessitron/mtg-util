spell-power-eval results

Which classifier agrees best with you?

Ranked by mean score error: the average distance between its 0 to 2 score and yours. Lower is better. Cost is an estimate: the tokens each API reported times cached prices, summed over the run. Runs written before cost was tracked show a dash.

Where do they disagree with you?

One column per classifier, one 0 to 2 axis in every cell. The ink tick is your score; the dot is the classifier's. The line between them is the miss.

Where do they miss in the same direction?

Signed error: the classifier's score minus yours. Blue rates the card weaker than you do, red rates it more powerful. The last row averages each column, so a lean in one direction shows up as bias.

Every answer

The same data as the charts, with each reason in full. The error is your score subtracted from the classifier's.

Confusion counts

How often each classifier answered a label (right of the arrow) when you said another (left of it). Labels are the scores rounded.
